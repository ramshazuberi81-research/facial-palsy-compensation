Measurements

Values are in inter-pupillary-distance (IPD) units. With one photo you get static left/right differences. With neutral and peak photos you also get movement per side and an asymmetry index, (healthy − palsy) ÷ (healthy + palsy) when the palsy side is set, otherwise |R − L| ÷ (R + L). Flag threshold 0.15 is a provisional value, not validated. Compensation versus a healthy norm is not shown: it needs validated control data.
