# Validation plan (summary)

1. **Feasibility on public data (4–6 weeks):** healthy-control norms (NeuroFace), repeat-capture reliability (ICC), minimal detectable change, palsy vs. control separation, MediaPipe landmark error vs. manual landmarks.
2. **Clinical pilot (20–30 patients):** pair the index with Sunnybrook or eFACE grades at several visits; test whether healthy-side excess adds information beyond symmetry (patient-level cross-validation, bootstrap CI on the AUC difference).
3. **Dose-response (clinician-led):** before/after images and injection maps; only after steps 1–2 look reliable.
4. **Optional:** relate video measures to sEMG.

**Continue if:** the index is reliable on public data, a clinician commits to graded patients, and a literature search finds no existing compensation measure.
**Park if:** the index stays noisy, no clinician commits within about 2 months, or a published tool already does this.

**Regulatory:** keep outputs as measurement and decision support. Dose suggestions or triage advice may fall under medical-device rules (FDA, CE, DRAP).
